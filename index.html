Built Different by Design

Explore the signature features that made Ghosted the talk of the campus.

⏳

The 24h Decay Matrix

Whispers don't live forever. As posts age toward their 24-hour limit, Gaussian blur algorithms gradually fade the text and media until they dissolve into dust. Zero digital permanence.

  • Adaptive optical text decay
  • Cloud Firestore TTL document auto-purging
  • Community Echo system to extend vital threads
🕯️

Séance & Hauwa Confessions

A hidden portal accessible exclusively by tapping the Ghost glyph on mobile. Students uncover a sanctuary for heartfelt, wax-sealed confessions, sorted by categories with candle-lighting reactions.

  • Custom digital wax seals & mood stamps
  • Category filtering (#crush, #exams, #hostel)
  • Anonymous comment resonance threads
🎬

Happy Watch Synchronized Party

Turned dorm rooms into synchronized cinemas. Watch YouTube videos, music premieres, or study playlists together with ultra-low latency playback sync and floating spectator reactions.

  • Sub-50ms room state synchronizer
  • In-room live spectator reaction physics
  • Anonymous callsign participant roster
💬

Vapor Chat & View-Once Photos

Direct & group chats where every message bubble is a vapor particle. Send encrypted view-once photos that self-destruct 5 seconds after inspection with no screenshot footprint.

  • 5-second self-destructing media viewer
  • Group chats with custom anonymous admin keys
  • Automatic midnight cleanup of chat trees
📖

Yearbook & Spectral Resonance

Gamified anonymous identity. Earn Resonance XP by actively contributing to the campus void. Ascend the spectral hierarchy from humble Phantom to legendary Void Sovereign.

  • Dynamic department badges (ICT, Design, Engg)
  • Daily login streak bonus resonance multipliers
  • Zero name or phone number linkages
🎨

Quad-Theme Dynamic Engine

Engineered with 4 custom visual design languages: Ghosted Ember Orange, Cosmic Purple Nebula, Aurora Borealis Teal, and Graphic Novel Comic Panel.

  • Instant runtime CSS / Canvas theme swap
  • Adaptive particle background simulations
  • Custom typography matching each mood

The Zero-Footprint Campus Privacy Shield

To eliminate social anxiety and ensure genuine student safety, Ghosted was engineered without user tracking, phone number requirements, or permanent message stores. All content was strictly ephemeral with automated 24-hour document disposal.
